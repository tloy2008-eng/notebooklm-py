What Is Compliance & Ethics?
Definitions, importance & global frameworks
📚 Definitions
Compliance is the act of conforming to rules, policies, laws and regulations governing an organisation's conduct.
Ethics is the moral framework guiding decisions and conduct — choosing right over wrong even when unmonitored.
🏏
Importance in Sports Governance
Preserves integrity of competition · Protects the welfare of athletes and officials · Maintains public trust and sponsorship confidence · Prevents sanctions from ICC and international bodies
🌐
ICC & National Expectations
ICC Integrity Framework sets mandatory standards for all member associations on anti-corruption, safeguarding, and governance autonomy.

Nigerian Sports Governance requires alignment with the National Sports Commission and Sports Trust Fund regulations.
⚖️
Why It Matters to the Board
Board members carry personal accountability. Failure to oversee compliance can result in ICC suspension, loss of funding, reputational damage, and legal liability.

Conflict of Interest
Definition, examples and the declaration process
Definition: A conflict of interest arises when a personal, financial or other interest of an NCF official could — or could appear to — influence their objective judgment in carrying out NCF duties.
⚡ Common Examples
  • Board member awarding contract to own company
  • Selector choosing family member for national squad
  • Official accepting gifts from sponsors seeking contracts
  • Coach with financial stake in player's management agency
  • Board member with political ties influencing governance
📋 Declaration Process
Step 1 — Identify
Recognise that a personal interest may conflict with your NCF role before the relevant meeting or decision.
Step 2 — Declare
Submit a written declaration to the Compliance Officer or chairperson before the agenda item is discussed.
Step 3 — Recuse
Withdraw from the discussion and any vote on the matter. Leave the meeting room if required.
Step 4 — Record
The declaration and recusal are minuted and retained on the conflict of interest register.

🔍 Assessment Phase

🔎 Investigation Phase
Assigned to an independent investigator or the Ethics Committee. Evidence collection, confidential interviews, and a 30-day timeline (extendable). Updates provided every 14 days to the reporter.

⚡ Escalation Triggers
Serious violations are escalated to the ICC Anti-Corruption Unit or Nigerian law enforcement. The annual audit reviews process effectiveness and drives policy improvement.

Case Study: Match Manipulation
Pakistan spot-fixing scandal — Lord's Test, August 2010
🇵🇰
Pakistan Spot-Fixing Scandal (2010)
Mohamamd Amir · Mohammad Asif · Salman Butt · Lord's Test vs England
● CORRUPTION
⚡ What Happened
Bookmakers bribed players to bowl deliberate no-balls at pre-agreed moments. A News of the World sting operation recorded the deal. Players were arrested, tried in the UK, jailed and given 5–7 year ICC bans.
🚨 Issues & Impact
  • Breach of trust with fans & sponsors
  • Criminal charges & imprisonment
  • Tarnished image of Pakistan cricket
  • Undermined integrity of international cricket
✅ NCF Preventive Measures
  • Robust anti-corruption code with strict penalties
  • Mandatory ICC ACU education for all players
  • AI-driven monitoring of suspicious betting patterns
  • Confidential reporting hotline for bookmaker approaches
  • Financial counselling to reduce vulnerability to bribes
🔑 NCF Lesson: Even a single incident of match manipulation can result in lifelong reputational damage, ICC sanctions, and criminal prosecution. All players and officials must report any approach immediately to the Compliance Officer or ICC Anti-Corruption Unit.

Case Study: Conflict of Interest
BCCI — The Greg Chappell Era (2005–2007)
🇮🇳
BCCI — Coach Influence on Team Selection
Greg Chappell · Selection controversy · Removal of Sourav Ganguly & other seniors (2005)
● GOVERNANCE
⚡ What Happened
Coach Greg Chappell was alleged to have improperly influenced selectors to drop senior players including Sourav Ganguly. The absence of clear role boundaries led to public disputes, eroding team morale and BCCI's credibility. Chappell departed in 2007.
🚨 Issues & Impact
  • Erosion of public trust in selection process
  • Compromised team performance
  • Lack of transparency and accountability
  • Public fallout between coach and players
✅ NCF Preventive Measures
  • Clear written mandate — coach is advisory, not selector
  • Independent selection committee with fixed staggered terms
  • Published, transparent selection criteria
  • Mandatory CoI declaration before selection meetings
  • Ethics committee to investigate selection complaints
🔑 NCF Lesson: Unclear role boundaries are a governance risk. NCF must define and publish selection criteria, separate the coach and selector roles, and mandate conflict-of-interest declarations for all decision-making bodies.

Case Study: Harassment & Safeguarding
Cricket Australia — The 2018 Sanderson Affair
🇦🇺
Cricket Australia — Workplace Harassment (2018)
James Sutherland · Dianna Brooks · Independent review & CEO resignation
● SAFEGUARDING
⚡ What Happened
A former CA employee raised harassment complaints in 2013. The inadequate response by CA leadership led to the employee's departure in 2014. By 2018, an independent review exposed systemic failures, prompting the CEO's resignation and a public reckoning over gender equality in Australian sport.
🚨 Issues & Impact
  • Hostile workplace — culture of silence and fear
  • Inadequate response to complaints
  • Major reputational damage for CA
  • National conversation on gender safety in sport
✅ NCF Preventive Measures
  • Zero-tolerance harassment policy with clear process
  • External, confidential reporting system
  • Dedicated safeguarding officer — trained specialist
  • Mandatory bystander intervention training for all staff
  • Counselling and legal support for victims
  • Diverse boards and committees to enhance inclusivity
🔑 NCF Lesson: Inadequate response to a single complaint can escalate into systemic scandal. NCF must ensure every complaint is handled promptly, transparently and with full support for the reporter, at every level of management.

Case Study: Governance Breakdown
Zambia Cricket (2019) & Sri Lanka Cricket (2023)
🇿🇲
Zambia Cricket Union — Suspended 2019
Non-compliance · Expulsion 2021 · Reinstated 2025
Issues
Failed ICC governance standards · Weak institutional frameworks · Lack of transparency · Barred from all ICC events for 6 years.
Fix: Governance Overhaul
  • Clear policies aligned with ICC standards
  • Administrator training on governance & compliance
  • Independent oversight & regular audits
🇱🇰
Sri Lanka Cricket — Suspended Nov 2023
Government interference · ICC Article 2.4(D) breach · World Cup relocated
Issues
Government dissolved SLC board after poor World Cup performance. Violated ICC autonomy rules. Lost funding, suspended from events, Under-19 World Cup relocated to South Africa.
Fix: Autonomy & Legal Reforms
  • New sports law reducing ministerial powers
  • Governance structure aligned with ICC standards
  • Stakeholder dialogue: ICC + government + SLC
🔑 NCF Lesson: Government interference in NCF governance, or failure to meet ICC membership criteria, risks suspension from international cricket with severe financial and reputational consequences. The Board must champion and protect NCF's constitutional autonomy.

Compliance in Event & Match Management
Pre-match integrity controls and monitoring obligations
📋 Pre-Match Compliance Checks
  • Verify all player eligibility documentation
  • Brief players on reporting duty — any approach from bookmakers
  • Confirm anti-doping officer is on-site and testing plan is in place
  • Check accreditation of all match officials and media

  • Review unusual patterns in player or official behaviour

  • Prohibit mobile phones in certain areas during play
  • Record all unusual occurrences in the match integrity log
  • Report concerns immediately to the Compliance Officer

Anti-Doping Obligations
ICC & WADA compliance requirements
NCF is obligated to enforce the ICC Anti-Doping Code (aligned with the World Anti-Doping Code — WADA) for all players participating in NCF-sanctioned competitions.
📋 Key NCF Obligations
  • Implement and enforce the ICC Anti-Doping Code
  • Conduct in-competition and out-of-competition testing
  • Provide whereabouts information for targeted testing
  • Educate all players on prohibited substances list
  • Report positive tests to ICC within required timeframe
  • Impose mandatory sanctions for anti-doping violations
⚗️
Prohibited Substances
WADA annually publishes the prohibited list covering stimulants, narcotics, steroids, peptide hormones, beta-blockers, diuretics and masking agents. Therapeutic use exemptions (TUEs) apply.
🚫
Sanctions
First violation: 4-year ban (intentional doping). Presence/use of prohibited substance: 2-year ban. Repeated violations: lifetime ban. All results from that period may be disqualified.
📚
Education Requirement
Annual anti-doping education is mandatory for all NCF-registered players and support personnel. Records of training must be maintained for ICC audit purposes.

Consequences of Non-Compliance
What is at stake for NCF, individuals and Nigerian cricket
🚫
Disciplinary Action
Warnings and formal reprimands · Suspension from duties · Termination of employment or role · Bans from cricket at all levels · Financial penalties and recovery of funds
🌍
ICC Escalation
Referral to ICC Anti-Corruption Unit · Suspension of NCF membership · Loss of ICC Development Fund grants · Removal from international events · Reputational damage affecting future investment
⚖️
Legal Consequences
Criminal prosecution for fraud or corruption · Civil liability for negligence or breach of duty · Regulatory action by the Nigerian Sports Commission · Directors' personal liability for governance failures
💔 Real-World Impact
  • Nigerian cricket excluded from ICC events
  • Players unable to represent Nigeria internationally
  • Loss of sponsors and commercial revenue
  • Destruction of decades of grassroots cricket development
✅ How to Avoid This
  • Attend and complete all mandatory compliance training
  • Report suspected violations immediately — don't wait
  • Declare all conflicts of interest before every meeting
  • Support investigations rather than impede them
